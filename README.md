## About

Second-year CSE undergrad at SGGSIE&T, Nanded. Early in the stack, but deliberate about it: I'd rather understand why something works than memorise that it does.

Software development is the throughline — writing programs that are readable six months later, not just working today. Cybersecurity pulls me toward the other side of the same problem: once you've tried to break something, you write it differently. AI/ML is the part I'm most curious about right now, so most of my "spare time" project work lives there.

Open source is how I'm learning to read other people's code, and problem solving — DSA, puzzles, the occasional contest — is the daily habit that keeps the fundamentals sharp.
